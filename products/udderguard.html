Herd Health Challenges

Better records. Healthier cows. More confident decisions.

The Challenge

Mastitis cases are difficult to track

When case details and treatment histories are scattered, farmers can struggle to see which cows need attention and whether previous infections are recurring.

  • ✕ Missed follow-ups Without clear reminders, important rechecks can be overlooked during busy milking routines.
  • ✕ Recurring infections go unnoticed Repeated mastitis cases in the same cow can be difficult to identify without an accessible history.
  • ✕ SCC results lack context Somatic Cell Count results are harder to act on when they are not considered alongside individual cow health records.
The Solution

Bring your herd health records together

UdderGuard gives dairy farmers and veterinarians a shared, clear view of mastitis cases, treatment histories, SCC results and outstanding follow-ups.

  • ✓ Keep every case organised Record mastitis cases against individual cows and maintain accessible treatment histories for better continuity.
  • ✓ Identify cows needing attention Highlight high-risk cows, unresolved cases and recurring infections so attention can be directed where it matters.
  • ✓ Stay on top of rechecks Schedule follow-up reminders and monitor case outcomes to support consistent herd health management.
